Extranet

An extranet is a private network that extends an intranet to trusted external users, such as partners, suppliers, or customers.

Extranet uses

  • Secure collaboration with outside organizations.
  • Shared access to documents, data, and services.
  • Often protected by VPNs, firewalls, and user authentication.
